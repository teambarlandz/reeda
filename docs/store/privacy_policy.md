# Reeda Privacy Policy

> Host this page (GitHub Pages or own domain) before the Play release and
> link it in the Play listing + in-app Settings → About (RELEASE.md §7).
> Version 1.0 — effective on first Play release (v1.0.x).

## 1. What we collect

**Nothing.** Reeda v1.0 collects no personal data, has no accounts, no
analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no telemetry. There is no network
permission in the app: it cannot send or receive data.

## 2. Where your data lives

All data — imported books, highlights, notes, reading progress and the
search index — is stored locally on your device only.

## 3. Backups

If you enable Android device backups, Google may back up your database,
books and covers. The search index is excluded and rebuilt automatically.
You can disable app backups at any time in Android settings.

## 4. Text-to-speech

Read-aloud uses Android's built-in text-to-speech engine. If the engine
offers networked voices, any speech data leaves your device only
according to that engine's own policies (typically none is sent).

## 5. Crash reporting (future)

Reeda v1.0 has no crash reporting. If an anonymous opt-in crash reporter
is introduced in a future version (v1.1+), it will collect only a stack
trace, app version and device class, be fully deletable, and this policy
will be updated before it ships.

## 6. Permissions

Reeda requests no permissions beyond the file picker you use to choose
books, and playback controls for read-aloud. Files you open are read only
at your request and are copied into the app's private storage.
