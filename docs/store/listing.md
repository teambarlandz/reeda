## Full description

```
Reeda is a private, offline ebook reader for EPUB and PDF. No accounts,
no ads, no tracking — your books and notes stay on your device.

Features:
• Read aloud (text-to-speech) with adjustable speed
• Highlight in 4 colors and add notes; export highlights to Markdown
• Full-text search inside your library
• EPUB & PDF import from any file picker or share menu
• 5 reading themes and adjustable font sizes
• DRM-free only — works with your own EPUB/PDF files
• No permissions required beyond opening files you choose

Reeda does not collect, upload, or sell any data. Imported books are
stored locally and can be backed up to Google (your database, books and
covers only — the search index is rebuilt automatically).

Requirements: Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer.
```
